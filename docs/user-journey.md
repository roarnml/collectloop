# User Journey

## Primary persona

An independent service provider who needs to collect a deposit and later collect
the remaining balance from a customer.

## Business-owner journey

1. Open the business dashboard.
2. Create a payment request with customer, service, amount, and due date.
3. Share the customer-facing request.
4. Monitor payment status.
5. Review paid and outstanding balances.

## Customer journey

1. Open the shared request.
2. Review recipient, purpose, amount, and payment information.
3. Select the compatible payment option presented by the supported environment.
4. Complete the required authorization.
5. View the resulting status.

## Success condition

A provider-confirmed payment is correctly associated with its request, and the
business view reflects the correct paid and outstanding amounts.

## Research questions

- How do microbusinesses currently request payments?
- How do they confirm that a customer has paid?
- How do they track deposits and balances?
- What happens when customers pay in instalments?
- What causes the most manual work or uncertainty?
